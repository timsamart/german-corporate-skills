## Criterion critique and limits

- v03-gv-01 assertion 1 combines a feasible replacement agenda with explicit diagnosis of the previous agenda. These are separate observable behaviors. Keep the original criterion intact for the current grade; in a future evaluation, split them if each behavior needs an independently interpretable result. Otherwise, do not count a feasible replacement alone as satisfying the diagnosis.
- v03-pr-01 assertion 1 names the investment committee on 5 November, while the source describes a four-week pilot decision on 14 October. Even a pilot beginning immediately after that decision would finish after 5 November. The output reasonably directs the 50,000-Euro pilot decision to the portfolio committee, assigns the 240,000-Euro introduction to the investment committee, and flags reconciliation of the 5 November date with pilot completion. This criterion passed on the connected paragraph's substantive allocation of decision and date, not on a verbatim phrase. A future source/criterion should distinguish the committee's next meeting date from an achievable post-pilot rollout decision date.
